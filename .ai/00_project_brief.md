# 00 - Project Brief: AI Trading Bot (MetaTrader 5 & Quantitative Engine)

## 1. Project Goal
Build an automated, production-ready AI quantitative trading bot integrating with MetaTrader 5 (MT5), utilizing SQLite for operational trade logging, signal tracking, risk management, and equity history, augmented with:
- **Price Action Law of Bulls and Bears (6 Market Cases A - F)**
- **Mathematical ML & Deep Learning Pipeline** (Logistic Regression, Sigmoid, Binary Cross-Entropy, Multi-Layer Perceptron with L2 Regularization & Inverted Dropout, Gaussian HMM for 3 Market Regimes, Monte Carlo VaR)
- **Anti-Noise & Out-of-Distribution (OOD) Filter** (Isolation Forest with Anomaly Scoring)
- **Dynamic 1R Lot Sizing & Custom Fixed Lot (v2) + 24h Circuit Breaker (`LOCK_BOT_24H()`) & Target Profit Circuit Breaker (`close_all_positions()`)**
- **Real-Time Bidirectional WebSocket Streaming** from backend engine to frontend dashboard.

## 2. Key Modules
- **Configuration (`config.py`)**: Environment-based credentials, path configuration, dynamic v2 runtime parameters (`INITIAL_CAPITAL`, `CUSTOM_LOT_SIZE`, `TARGET_PROFIT_GOAL`, `BOT_RUNNING`), and Spread/Commission/Swap/Slippage parameters (`MAX_ALLOWED_SPREAD`, `RISK_PER_TRADE = 0.01`, `COMMISSION_PER_LOT_USD = 7.0`, `COMMISSION_BUFFER_PIPS = 0.7`, `SLIPPAGE_BUFFER_PIPS = 0.3`, `MIN_FREE_MARGIN_RATIO = 0.15`, `SWAP_PER_LOT_DAILY_USD`, `USE_CUSTOM_LOT = False`).
- **Database (`db.py`)**: SQLite schema initialization and migration (tables: `orders`, `bot_signals`, `daily_equity`, `bot_logs`, `app_settings`).
- **MT5 Connector (`mt5_connector.py`)**: Terminal initialization, broker authentication, connection lifecycle management, `get_current_spread_pips(symbol)`, `get_contract_multiplier(symbol)`, `get_pip_value_per_lot(symbol, price)`, `calculate_rollover_swap()`, `get_position_swap_and_commission()`, `sync_closed_deal_from_mt5()`, and seamless Mock simulation on macOS/Linux (dynamically accounting for floating Spread, Round-Turn Commission, Rollover Swap, and Free Margin in `account_info()`).
- **Price Action Engine (`ai_price_action.py`)**: 6 Market Cases (Case A Trend, Case B Compression, Case C Broadening Chaos, Case D Liquidity Sweep, Case E Leading Wave, Case F Rollover News) and 3-condition Compression Detection.
- **Multi-Dataset Training Pool (`create_training_pool.py`, `data/hf_datasets/`)**: Combines 5 Hugging Face datasets (`arvind7754/btc-candlestick-dataset`, `tripolskypetr/tradingview-ideas-signals`, `jukik45/mt5_eurusd`, `akashkumar5/EURUSD-15M-RegimeBench`, `vichetkao/xauusd_dataset_2026` + 637 High-Impact CPI/NFP/FOMC News Events) and 20,000 MT5 Exness historical bars into a unified 105,262-row Mega Training Pool (`data/training_pool.csv`, `data/processed_dataset.parquet`, 36 features including `candle_bullish_score`, `tv_signal_confluence`, `regime_bench_score`, and `macro_news_safety_score`).
- **MQL5 MT5 Signals Crawler (`mql5_signal_crawler.py`, `data/mql5_signals/`)**: Dedicated crawler and feature engineering pipeline extracting 69+ metrics from live MT5 signals (Sharpe ratio, Profit Factor, Recovery Factor, Max Drawdown, Win Rate, holding times, equity curves) generating supervised AI training datasets (`mql5_signals_ai_features.parquet` & `mql5_signals_equity_curves.json`).
- **Mathematical AI Models (`ai_math_models.py`, `train_model.py`)**: LightGBM Gradient Boosting Classifier, Sigmoid activation, Vectorized Logistic Regression, Deep MLP with L2/Dropout, Gaussian HMM Regimes, Isolation Forest OOD filter, and Monte Carlo VaR.
- **Risk Manager (`risk_manager.py`)**: R:R ratio check (min 1:1.5, default 1:2.0), Custom Lot priority (`get_execution_lot()`), Commission & Slippage-inclusive 1R Lot sizing (`calculate_standard_lot()` & `calculate_lot_size_1r()`), Free Margin verification & lot capping (`check_free_margin_and_cap_lot()`), Commission Buffer Take Profit (`calculate_tp_with_buffer()`), Target Profit Circuit Breaker (`check_target_goal()` & `is_target_goal_reached()` reading real-time `equity`), and 24h lockout circuit breaker on 2 consecutive losses.
- **Executor (`executor.py`)**: Order execution and `close_all_positions()` with `deviation = 20`, Round-Turn Commission (`7.0 USD/lot`), and Rollover Swap deduction to immediately close all open MT5 positions when the target profit goal is reached.
- **Autonomous Live Engine (`ai_trading_engine.py`)**: Background loop continuously evaluating ticks, syncing closed deals from MT5 (`sync_closed_deal_from_mt5()`), checking the Target Profit Circuit Breaker, filtering real-time spread via `MAX_ALLOWED_SPREAD`, verifying Free Margin, executing trades with 1R Commission+Slippage Lot & TP Buffer, deducting Round-Turn Commission (`7.0 USD/lot`), Rollover Swap, Stop-Market Slippage, and Bid/Ask Spread in live PnL/Wallet updates, logging to `trading.log`, and broadcasting via WebSockets.
- **Web Terminal (`web_app.py`, `dashboard.js`, `index.html`)**: Real-time trading dashboard with Lightweight Charts, AI HUD, dynamic Bot v2 configuration form (`/api/start_bot`, `/api/stop_bot`), manual order placement/closing wired directly to `MT5Executor` with Max Spread & Free Margin checks, and live VNĐ/USD wallet.

## 3. Supported Environments
- **Primary Runtime for MT5 Execution**: Windows (Windows 10/11 or Windows Server VPS) where MetaTrader 5 Terminal 64-bit is installed.
- **Development & Analysis**: Cross-platform (macOS / Linux / Windows) with SQLite, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, LightGBM, and hmmlearn.
